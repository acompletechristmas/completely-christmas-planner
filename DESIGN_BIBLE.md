# A Complete Christmas — Design Bible v1.0

> **How to use:** Begin every future prompt with:
> *"Follow the A Complete Christmas Design Bible v1.0. This document is the source of truth for design and UX. Do not introduce new colours, layouts, components or interaction styles that conflict with it. If a requested feature requires a change to these standards, explain the conflict before making the change."*

---

## Mission

A Complete Christmas exists to **remove the stress from Christmas while increasing the magic**.

Every page should make the user feel:
> *"Christmas is organised. Christmas is exciting. Christmas starts here."*

The website must feel like opening a beautifully wrapped Christmas planner.

It must **never** feel like: business software, project management software, spreadsheets, accounting software, or a generic SaaS application.

## Brand Personality

**Always:** Magical · Warm · Premium · Elegant · Calm · Cosy · Organised · Timeless · Welcoming

**Never:** childish · cluttered · corporate · overly technical · AI generated · cold · clinical

## Visual Inspiration

John Lewis Christmas · Fortnum & Mason Christmas · Liberty Christmas · Disney Christmas (subtle elegance, not cartoons).

## Colour Palette

- **Midnight Christmas Blue** — primary background, deep navy with soft gradients. Used on almost every page.
- **Snow White** — planner cards, forms, gift cards, information panels. Cards should feel like premium stationery.
- **Champagne Gold** — borders, icons, buttons, headings, highlights, completion states. Never harsh yellow — always warm champagne.
- **Forest Green** — used sparingly for success, nature, Christmas accents.
- **Rich Christmas Red** — only for ribbons, bows, seals, festive highlights. Avoid bright red as a general UI colour.

## Backgrounds

Most planner pages should include: subtle snowfall, tiny stars, soft sparkles, evergreen branches, gentle lighting. Animation must always stay subtle — content is the focus.

## Cards

Cards are one of the defining features of the website.

- Warm white background
- Soft rounded corners
- Thin champagne gold border
- Generous spacing
- Subtle shadow
- Faint embossed snowflake pattern (barely visible — like luxury embossed Christmas stationery)

## Typography

- **Elegant serif** for: page titles, section titles, person names.
- **Clean sans-serif** for: descriptions, prices, buttons, labels, progress, navigation.
- Headings must always be visually stronger than body text.

## Layout

Every page follows this structure:
1. Large page heading
2. Short friendly introduction
3. Primary action buttons
4. Main content
5. Secondary content

Use generous white space. Nothing should feel cluttered.

## Navigation

Every page uses the same global header:
- A Complete Christmas logo (always returns to Home)
- Hamburger menu
- Consistent navigation

## Buttons

Rounded corners · gold outlines or gold fill · generous padding · tactile feel · gentle hover and press animations. Avoid tiny text links where a button would improve usability.

## Borders

Elegant outlines around buttons, cards, forms, sections. Borders help organise the interface.

## Icons

Elegant line icons only. **Never** cartoon icons, emoji as interface icons, or AI-style illustrations. Icons should be simple and consistent.

## Animations

**Use:** snowfall · sparkle once · gentle fades · ribbon appearing · soft hover effects.
**Avoid:** bouncing · spinning · excessive movement.

## People & Presents (heart of the site)

The user should instantly understand:
- Who am I buying for?
- What have I bought?
- What still needs buying?
- What is wrapped?
- What is finished?

Never require unnecessary clicks.

### Person Card States

| Stage | Trigger | Visual |
|---|---|---|
| **Default** | Has presents but not all bought | White background, subtle snowflake texture, gold outline |
| **All Bought** | Every present ordered | Champagne gold card, sparkle animation once, badge: `✓ All presents bought` |
| **All Wrapped** | Every present wrapped | Keep gold background, add luxurious red satin ribbon + gold-edged satin bow, label: `Ready for Christmas` |
| **All Sent/Given** | Every present sent or given | Keep gold + ribbon, add wax seal: `Christmas Complete` (traditional sealing wax feel) |

### Snowflakes

Signature design element:
- Embossed snowflakes on white cards
- Slightly brighter snowflakes on gold cards
- Falling snow in backgrounds

Snowflakes must never interfere with readability.

### Gift Tags

Completed cards may display a handwritten-style gift tag, e.g. *For Lauren ❤️* — enhances the premium gift-wrapping feel.

## Progress

Progress should be rewarding — users should feel they are gradually completing Christmas. Each completed stage should feel satisfying. Avoid progress systems that feel like office software.

## Empty States

Never show a blank page. Use beautiful festive illustrations and encouraging messages, e.g.
> *"Let's make Christmas magical. Add your first person to begin."*

## Writing Style

Warm · reassuring · encouraging · concise. Avoid technical, corporate, or robotic wording.

## Mobile First

Every page designed for mobile before desktop. Target widths: **360px** and **390px**. Buttons easy to tap. No horizontal scrolling. No hidden controls.

## Accessibility

Maintain good contrast. Do not rely on colour alone to show status. Support keyboard navigation. Use meaningful labels for controls.

## Golden Rule

Every screen must answer within five seconds:
> *"Do I immediately know what to do next?"*

If the answer is no, simplify the design.

## Final Design Principle

Every page should contain **one small moment of Christmas magic** — a subtle sparkle, a snowflake, a satin ribbon, a wax seal, a beautifully written message. These moments should make the user smile without distracting them from planning Christmas.
